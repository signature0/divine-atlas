# NORSE MYTHOLOGY — CULTURE-SPECIFIC BRIEF
Framing: historical mythology of pre-Christian Scandinavia, preserved almost entirely in LATER written sources (Iceland, 13th c.). Emphasize: the myths are older than the texts; Snorri was writing ~1000 years after the Viking Age. Distinguish Eddic (poetic Edda) vs. Prose Edda vs. sagas.
Primary sources: Poetic Edda (Codex Regius, ca. 1270), Prose Edda (Snorri Sturluson, 1220s), Volsunga saga, Hrafnagaldsgata, sagas of heroes, Younger Futhark runestones (Gundestrup is Celtic — no; use e.g. Gripsholm runestone, Rök stone). Translations: Sacred Texts Project, Grimoire/Edda.org, University of Oxford resources.
Core entities (must include): Ginnungagap (as primordial place/concept — classify carefully), Ymir, Audhumla, Buri, Bor, Odin, Vili, Ve, Mimir, Bestla, Baldr, Nanna, Hodr, Thor, Sif, Loki, Hel (ruler of the dead), Njord, Freyr, Freyja, Skadi, Tyr, Heimdall, Vidar, Vali, Nidhogg, Jormungandr, Fenrir, Surtr, Niflheim/Muspelheim as realms not entities (keep in cosmology), Vafthrudnir (optional).
Critical variants to document:
- The three brothers Odin/Vili/Ve: Prose Edda genealogy (Bor's sons) — some scholars treat Vili and Ve as poetic epithets of Odin (variant, disputed).
- Odin's parents: Bor (standard) — note the genealogical oddities scholars flag.
- Hel: daughter of Loki + Angrboda (standard); her "half dead, half alive" nature.
- Freyja's parentage: Njord (standard) vs. some saga traditions where Freyja and Freyr are Odin's children.
- Mimir's role: god vs. figure — note scholarly caution.
Realms: the Nine Worlds — Midgard, Asgard, Vanaheim, Alfheim, Jotunheim, Svartalfheim/Nidavellir, Niflheim, Muspelheim, Helheim — all hanging on Yggdrasil.
Museum art: NO contemporary Norse originals survive (oral tradition). Artwork = 19th-c. Romantic illustrations (Munch, Wadmal), Viking Age metalwork/reliefs (Oseberg tapestries — Met, National Museum Denmark, Swedish History Museum), Gokstad/Oseberg finds, runestones. Munch works: many in National Gallery Oslo (Nasjonalgallet) — verify public-domain status (Munch d. 1914 → PD in Norway/US for pre-1929 works).
Mandatory myths: Creation from Ymir, Odin and the runes (self-sacrifice on Yggdrasil), Loki's children, Death of Baldr, Ragnarok. Add: Thor's journeys / Hymir, Thor and the giants (Heimsdalr), Vafthrudnir's quiz (optional).
