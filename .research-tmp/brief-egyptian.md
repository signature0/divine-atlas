# ANCIENT EGYPTIAN RELIGION/MYTHOLOGY — CULTURE-SPECIFIC BRIEF
Framing: historical religious tradition of ancient Egypt (c. 3000 BCE – 300 BCE, with continuations into Roman period). NOT a "mythology" in the same sense as Greek/Norse — it was a living state religion for 3000 years. Note this framing.
Primary sources: Pyramid Texts (oldest religious texts, c. 2400 BCE), Coffin Texts (c. 2000 BCE), Book of the Dead (c. 1550 BCE), Memphite Theology (Shabaka Stone), Hymn to the Amun, various temple inscriptions. Translations: British Museum, Met, various university resources.
CRITICAL: Multiple creation traditions coexisted in different cities/dynasties. Do NOT present one universal Egyptian genealogy. Document:
- Heliopolitan (Ennead): Atum → Shu + Tefnut → Geb + Nut → Osiris, Isis, Set, Nephthys, Horus
- Memphite: Ptah creates by thought/word
- Hermopolitan (Ogdoad): four male-female primal pairs (Khepri, Tefnut, etc.)
- Theban/Luxor: Amun as hidden creator
Core entities (must include): Atum/Ra (note the merger), Shu, Tefnut, Geb, Nut, Osiris, Isis, Set, Nephthys, Horus, Hathor, Thoth, Anubis, Bastet, Sekhmet, Sobek, Khepri, Ptah, Amun, Ma'at, Apep/Apep (Apophis), the Ogdoad (at least 2-3 pairs), Taweret (optional).
Critical variants to document:
- Ra vs. Atum vs. Amun: the merger (Amun-Ra) is a historical theological development, not a single entity.
- Horus: the "Horus" who kills Set is Horus the Elder (son of Osiris/Isis) vs. the infant Horus (son of Osiris/Isis in the Osiris cycle) — scholarly distinction.
- Isis's role: in some traditions she is Osiris's sister AND wife; in others just sister.
- Set: villain in the Osiris cycle, but was originally a protector of Ra's solar barque — role shifted over time.
- Ma'at: principle vs. deity — note the ambiguity.
Realms: the living world (Kemet), the sky (Nut), the Duat (underworld), the celestial realm, the solar barque's journey (Day boat + Night boat through the Duat).
Museum art: Egyptian art is mostly ancient (3000-300 BCE) — virtually all PUBLIC DOMAIN. Met Egyptian collection (Open Access), British Museum (Rosetta Stone, various), Louvre (Nefertiti bust is in Berlin — Altes Museum), Egyptian Museum Cairo (Tutankhamun). Tomb paintings: Valley of the Kings (verify photography license — many are restricted; use Wikimedia or Met). Papyrus: Book of the Dead papyri (Met, British Museum, Chester Beatty Dublin).
Mandatory myths: The murder of Osiris, Isis restoring Osiris, Horus vs Set (contested succession), Ra's journey through the Duat, the creation traditions (document as variants, not one story). Add: the Ennead creation, the Ogdoad, the solar barque journey.
