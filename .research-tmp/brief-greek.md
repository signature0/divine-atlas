# GREEK MYTHOLOGY — CULTURE-SPECIFIC BRIEF
Framing: historical mythology of ancient Greece (c. 12th c. BCE – 5th c. CE). Literary sources (Homer, Hesiod, Orphic hymns, tragedians, later mythographers like Apollodorus, Hyginus). Roman continuations (Ovid) are "later tradition" — note them, don't merge silently.
Primary sources to anchor on: Iliad, Odyssey, Theogony, Works and Days, Homeric Hymns, Orphic Hymns, Apollodorus Bibliotheca, Hyginus Fabulae, Pindar, Hesiodic corpus, Pausanias. Translations: Perseus Digital Library (Tufts), Loeb Classical Library, Sacred Texts Project.
Core entities (must include): Chaos, Gaia, Uranus, Nyx, Erebus, Pontus, Ouranos's children — Cronus, Rhea, Oceanus, Titanides; Zeus, Hera, Poseidon, Hades, Demeter, Hestia; Athena, Apollo, Artemis, Ares, Aphrodite, Hephaestus, Hermes, Dionysus, Asclepius (optional), Persephone, Heracles, Prometheus, Typhon, Echidna (optional), Eris.
Critical parentage variants to document:
- Aphrodite: born from sea foam off Uranus's severed genitals (Hesiod) vs daughter of Zeus + Dione (Homer Iliad 5.370-378).
- Athena: born from Zeus's head (later tradition; not in Hesiod — there she is daughter of Metis) vs. other versions.
- Hades: son of Cronus/Rhea (standard) vs. Orphic versions where Hades is the FIRST-born and a primordial figure.
- Eros: primordial (Orphic, first being) vs. child of Aphrodite + Ares (later).
- Heracles' divinity: hero → Olympian (apotheosis) — mark the category shift.
Realms: Olympus, Earth, Underworld (Hades/Pluto), Tartarus, Oceanus/Thalassa.
Museum art priorities: Met Greek vases + sculpture (Open Access), British Museum (Elgin Marbles — note the Parthenon frieze), Altes Museum Berlin, Louvre (Venus de Milo — attribution disputed), Getty. Ukiyo... no — Greek vase paintings (red-figure, black-figure), Parthenon sculptures, Roman copies of Greek originals (Vatican, Louvre).
Mandatory myths: Creation (Hesiod Theogony), Titanomachy, Prometheus and the gift of fire, Persephone and the seasons, Odyssey, Trojan War. Add: Birth of Athena, Calypso/Echo (skip), Argonauts or Perseus.
